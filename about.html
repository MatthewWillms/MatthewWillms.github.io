About Me

Background

My interest in computers and technology began at a young age and continued to develop through personal experience with computer hardware, software, and troubleshooting. After graduating from high school in 2018, I purchased my first laptop, which gave me more opportunities to explore computers independently.

Over time, my interest expanded beyond using computers to understanding how they work. In 2024, I purchased my first desktop computer and later built my own system, gaining practical experience with hardware selection, compatibility, configuration, and troubleshooting.

These experiences contributed to my decision to pursue a diploma in Computer Information Technology at Lethbridge Polytechnic, where I have been developing skills in programming, networking, databases, and systems analysis.

My Approach to Technology

I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying problems, and finding practical solutions. Whether working with computer hardware, developing software, or learning a new technology, I value opportunities to build my knowledge through hands-on experience.

My background in agricultural, manufacturing, and mechanical environments has also influenced how I approach technical challenges. These experiences have reinforced the importance of adaptability, attention to detail, and understanding how individual components contribute to a larger system.

Personal Mission Statement

My goal is to continue developing my technical knowledge and problem-solving abilities while pursuing opportunities to apply them in practical environments.

I aim to approach new challenges with curiosity, adaptability, and a willingness to learn, building upon my existing experience while expanding my capabilities across different areas of information technology.
